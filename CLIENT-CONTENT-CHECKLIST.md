# GEN-Z website — client content checklist

This checklist covers the real information and assets that would let the website move from a polished shop showcase to a more complete customer-ready store. Items marked **Priority** are the most useful next inputs.

## 1. Product and stock information

- [ ] **Priority:** Clear, current product photos for clothing, footwear, accessories and perfumes (ideally a few close-ups and full-product shots per category).
- [ ] **Priority:** Brand names and exact product names that the shop is happy to publish; confirm which items are currently in stock before we list them.
- [ ] **Priority:** Price ranges or individual selling prices, including whether prices vary by size or model.
- [ ] **Priority:** Available clothing sizes, footwear sizes and colour options; note when stock is limited or changes frequently.
- [ ] Any best-sellers, new arrivals, seasonal edits or featured products to highlight on the home page.
- [ ] Confirm which product types should be added, removed or renamed in each category.

## 2. Ordering and customer policies

- [ ] **Priority:** Whether customers can order online or only enquire by WhatsApp / visit the shop.
- [ ] Delivery availability, service areas, courier partners, delivery charges and expected timelines (if delivery is offered).
- [ ] Payment methods accepted in-store and for remote orders.
- [ ] Exchange, return, refund and cancellation rules, including time limits and product exclusions.
- [ ] Product-care advice, warranty information or after-sales support details, where relevant.

## 3. Shop and contact details

- [ ] **Priority:** Confirm the exact public address, landmark and Google Maps pin.
- [ ] **Priority:** Confirm weekday and weekend hours, plus holiday / festival exceptions and temporary closures.
- [ ] **Priority:** Verify the public phone / WhatsApp number and that click-to-call and WhatsApp links reach the right person.
- [ ] Confirm the official Instagram handle(s), including whether the separate perfume account should be shown.
- [ ] Any additional official channels the client wants linked (for example, another social profile).

## 4. Brand assets and marketing

- [ ] High-resolution logo files (SVG or transparent PNG), preferred brand colours and any font or usage guidelines.
- [ ] Permission-cleared photos of the store, team, new collections and customer styling.
- [ ] Current promotions, launch offers, bundle deals or loyalty benefits, with exact terms and expiry dates.
- [ ] Any brand badges, seller / reseller information or authenticity documentation the client is authorised to publish.
- [ ] Short brand introduction and the tone the client wants the store to use in its own words.

## 5. Trust, legal and launch approval

- [ ] Permission to display customer testimonials and any customer photos; confirm wording before publishing.
- [ ] Confirm that the Google rating / review count and review snippets currently shown are still accurate.
- [ ] Business name and any required legal / billing details for the website, if online selling is introduced.
- [ ] Approved privacy notice, terms, shipping policy and return policy if the site starts collecting customer information or taking orders.
- [ ] Final review of all product claims, store details, contact links and mobile / desktop layouts before launch.

**Suggested next step:** ask the client first for the current product/price/size sheet, a verified contact-and-hours sheet, and a fresh set of product photos. Those three inputs unlock the most useful next website improvements.
